Pronouns: He/him
Species: Dwarf

Bombur Baralkin is a dwarven alchemist with a passion for brewing high end meads and rums. He works in the pub in the [[The Kauri Station]], selling his alcohol to keep spirits up. He also manufactures serums for the medical station, as well as explosives and other useful equipment for the [[The Wildwood Research Commission]]. Bombur is long time friends with [[Olgon Arval]], making use of some of his foraged ingredients for his own creations - and trading him free ales in return.

Bombur is small and round, with rosy cheeks and a beard that falls to his waist.
