Pronouns: He/him - Species: Human

Often called "Chef Olgon" by the Kauri crew, Olgon Arval is an especially talented chef, known for his versatility in ingredient selection. His background in plant and foliage research at the [[The Pine Harbor Guild]] means he's especially good at foraging when resources - or just seasonings - get a little tight. While Olgon always loves a good laugh, and sometimes a good fight, his main focus will always be his food. Long time friends with [[Bombur Baralkin]].

Olgon is stocky and muscular, his black (but graying) hair always tied back in a bandana.
