The Kauri Station is a research camp on the outskirts of [[The Wildwood]], made up primarily of wooden buildings on either side of a small river and up against a cliff face; the river flows  from a waterfall coming down the cliff, and a scaffolded ramp leads up the cliff face to the observatory. The camp is occupied by members of [[The Wildwood Research Commission]].

**Buildings:**
Barracks
- Sleeping quarters for the residents of the station

Observatory
- Circular structure with a large wooden telescope sticking out of the roof. Its use is restricted to trained individuals.
	- Characters: Unknown

Supplies station/warehouse
- Storage for food, adventuring equipment, and other items. Also houses a workshop for tinkering with malfunctioning [[Mechanica]].
	- Characters: [[Freia Orland]]

Studies/offices
- A series of connected buildings where researchers work on their studies and investigate samples. 
	- Characters: [[Kurem Shaplan]], [[Erina Hawes]]

The mess hall/The Pub
- A combined dining and drinking location. Keeps spirits up.
	- Characters: [[Olgon Arval]], [[Bombur Baralkin]]

The medical center
- A small building with several beds for resting disease and injuries.
